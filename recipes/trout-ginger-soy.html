Pan-Seared Trout with Ginger-Soy Sauce

4 servings · Prep 15 min · Cook 15 min · Total 30 min

A lower-calorie, high-protein trout dinner. The Thermomix prepares the sauce while the trout is pan-seared last so it reaches the table hot.

Approximate nutrition: 330 kcal and 35-40 g protein per serving, depending on fillet size and sauce used.

Ingredients

  • 4 trout fillets, about 150-180 g each
  • 1 tsp neutral oil
  • 15 g fresh ginger, peeled
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 30 g low-sodium soy sauce
  • 20 g rice vinegar
  • 15 g maple syrup or honey
  • 10 g lime juice
  • 30 g water
  • 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds, optional

Preparation

  1. Pat the trout fillets dry. Season lightly with pepper; add little or no salt because the sauce contains soy sauce.
  2. Add ginger and garlic to the Thermomix mixing bowl. Chop 5 sec / speed 7. Scrape down the bowl.
  3. Add soy sauce, rice vinegar, maple syrup or honey, lime juice, water and sesame oil. Mix 10 sec / speed 4.
  4. Cook the sauce 4 min / 90°C / speed 1, measuring cup off. Transfer to a small serving jug and keep warm.
  5. Heat a large non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat. Add 1 tsp neutral oil.
  6. Place trout skin-side down if skin-on. Cook about 4-5 minutes, pressing gently for the first 20 seconds so the skin remains flat.
  7. Turn the fillets and cook about 1-3 minutes more, depending on thickness, until just cooked through and still moist.
  8. Plate immediately. Spoon a modest amount of warm ginger-soy sauce over each fillet and garnish with green onion and optional sesame seeds.
